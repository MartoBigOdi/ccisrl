# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JS (existing site, no framework). Served from the project root.

## Users
Private owners who want a house or building built in Buenos Aires and its country clubs, and companies that need offices, hotels, labs or industrial sheds built or refitted. They hire a firm to take a project from land evaluation to keys, and compare constructors on track record, scale and who they will deal with. (Inferred from the 2020 portfolio and the previous site; not interviewed.)

## Product Purpose
CCI Construcciones S.A.S. is a construction company with an in-house project office. The site's job is to win quote requests ("Pedir presupuesto") by showing the track record and the people behind it. Fictional client: the content is stand-in data for a portfolio exercise.

## Positioning
A constructor that also draws the executive plans and evaluates the land: one team from site evaluation to turnkey delivery, with 73 documented works since 1996, from 118 m² labs to a 35,000 m² site.

## Operating Context
Works are contracted turnkey ("llave en mano") or coordinated with the client's own supplies. Typical scales: houses 200 to 1,000 m², residential buildings 760 to 2,500 m², commercial and industrial up to 35,000 m². Currency of contracts is USD, one case in pesos.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Three services: Construcción, Oficina de proyectos, Desarrollos inmobiliarios. Language: Spanish (Argentina). Phones, emails and CPAU numbers appear as the 2026 PDF gives them; no invented hours or testimonials. The form sends by mailto to the firm's address. Photographs come from the 2020 PDF portfolio and are small (250 to 700 px wide); a few from the old site are larger (up to 1440 px).

## Brand Commitments
Name: CCI Construcciones S.A.S. (previously written Construcciones Civiles Integrales S.R.L.). People: arquitecto Roberto Prandi (founder), arquitecta Soledad Prandi (project manager). No existing logo beyond the name.

## Evidence on Hand
img/obras (photos extracted from the PDF), img/casas Martona, img/inversion*, img/plano*, portraits img/roberto.jpg and img/sole.jpg. All 73 works with year, m² and contract amount live in the 2026 PDF. No testimonials, awards or press: do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. Show the work before describing it.
2. Numbers from the portfolio are the proof; never invent figures.
3. One team from plans to keys.
4. Small source photos are used at sizes where they stay sharp.
